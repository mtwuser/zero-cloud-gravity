## 2. Verification & Non-Consideration Notice

Please review these three stewardship invariants before sending a contribution:

1. **Canonical Anti-Phishing Source**: The table in [`FUNDING.md`](FUNDING.md) on the `master` branch of [`mtwuser/zero-cloud-gravity`](https://github.com/mtwuser/zero-cloud-gravity) is the sole authoritative record of project patronage addresses. Project maintainers never solicit funds via private messages, social channels, or token launches.
2. **Unconditional Open-Source Patronage**: All contributions are voluntary, non-refundable gifts supporting public Open-Source engineering and documentation. Contributions do not constitute a commercial service contract, Service-Level Agreement (`SLA`), equity interest, token allocation, or preferential governance weight.
3. **Non-Financial Ways to Contribute**: Technical bug reports, architecture reviews, `1:1:1` unit/E2E test coverage, and curated public epistemic corpora (`gov-*`, `geo-*`, `sci-*`) remain equally valued under [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
